BiRD-02 · Closed-loop liveness

Coupling transport to the Monod cell engine at high biomass fires the reactor→cell feedback: dissolved O₂ drops below saturation as consumption outpaces transport, and recovers when biomass is removed (net d[O₂]/dt = transport − consumption).

High biomass settles DO at 1.49 mg/L (82% below C*); removing biomass recovers DO to 5.22 mg/L.
