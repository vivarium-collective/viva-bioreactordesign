BiRD-03 · Coupling-interval convergence
Claim: the reactor coupling/update interval is a tunable parameter, and the coupled dissolved-O₂ trajectory converges as the interval decreases (Δt → 0).
Provenance · composite viva_bioreactordesign.composites.coupled_reactor_cell (make_coupled_document, growth on, initial biomass 0.1 g/L, initial DO 8.0 mg/L) · common horizon 4 h · intervals Δt=0.08h (50 steps), Δt=0.04h (100 steps), Δt=0.02h (200 steps), Δt=0.01h (400 steps) · fine reference Δt=0.0025 h (1600 steps).
Sub-hour Δt: the transport term uses an explicit step, stable only for kLa·Δt ≲ 1 (kLa≈11/h ⇒ Δt≲ 0.09 h). Coarser intervals sit in the explicit-Euler overshoot regime and diverge — the very interval-dependence this study characterizes; convergence is shown in the stable regime, matching the study's acceptance test.
